About me

I'm Chloe Kilman, a physician who has built my career around caring for older adults. I am double board certified in Family Medicine and Geriatrics, and I am a Fellow of the American Academy of Family Physicians.

Today I work as a physician and Assistant Professor of Geriatrics at the University of Mississippi Medical Center. I am a Certified Dementia Practitioner and a Certified Montessori Dementia Care Professional. My work helps me understand what seniors and their families want and need, and I bring that into every care decision.

Caring for older adults is a privilege. My patients teach me something new every day, and I value the chance to learn from their lives and experiences.

That is why my husband, Nick Blanchard, and I founded Bloom Homes Assisted Living & Memory Care. I will serve as the Medical Director of Bloom Homes and lead the clinical side of our care. Nick leads the business side. Our first home, The Iris, opens in Spring 2027 at 112 Grants Ferry Rd. in Brandon. It is a purpose built memory care home with 16 private bedrooms, each with its own bathroom.

Bloom Homes Services

  • Assisted Living

    Help with daily tasks, medication management, and access to physical and occupational therapy in a small, 16 resident home.

  • Memory Care

    Memory care and dementia support with personalized care focused on dignity and quality of life.

  • Care Team

    24/7 on site care staff, physician oversight, with hospice and end of life support.

  • Daily Life & Amenities

    Private bedrooms and bathrooms, gardens, a library, a salon, meals from our in house chef, and daily activities like music, gardening, and seated exercise.

Phase 2: Homes 2 & 3

Phase 2 adds two more 16 bed homes on the same 4.57 acres. Each home will follow the same proven design and offer the same care and amenities as The Iris, while having its own unique character. True to the Bloom Homes brand, each home will be named after a flower. The flower names reflect our belief that every resident deserves a place to feel at home and bloom.

When all three homes are complete, Bloom Homes will serve up to 48 residents.
